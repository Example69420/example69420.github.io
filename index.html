👋 Welcome to SoundKey

SoundKey is a special audio interface built for Flipper Zero that makes the device easier to use for everyone. If you have low vision, or prefer listening rather than looking at small screens, SoundKey is here to help you. This software converts the Flipper Zero's visual interface into clear, spoken words and helpful sounds.

Created by Krishita Sanjay Choksi, SoundKey is free, open-source, and designed to make technology accessible to all. We believe that everyone should be able to use their devices with confidence and ease, regardless of their visual ability.

✨ Amazing Features

  • 🗣️ Spoken Feedback: Every action and menu item is read out loud to you
  • 🎵 Tone Guidance: Different tones tell you where you are in the menu
  • 📢 Simple Talking Menus: No more squinting at tiny text
  • 🔔 Clear Sound Cues: Understand your device with just your ears

  • 🎯 Accessibility First: Built with inclusion as the main goal, not an afterthought
  • 🔓 Completely Free: Open-source and available to everyone

🎯 How SoundKey Works

SoundKey adds a smart audio layer on top of the Flipper Zero's normal interface. When you move through menus, the device speaks the option names clearly. When you make a selection, you'll hear a confirmation sound. The background sounds change smoothly, so you can tell where you are just by listening. It's like having a friendly guide inside your device.

This software was tested with visually impaired and blind users to ensure it feels natural and helpful. Every sound was carefully chosen to be pleasant and easy to understand.
